# Outreach kit

Use this to share the list and the one-page handout with digital-security trainers and helplines. Nothing here requires an account or tracks recipients.

## Who to contact

| Audience | Why it fits | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Digital-security trainers | Need a vetted, printable reference for workshops | Access Now, Digital First Aid Kit contributors, local hackerspaces |
| Helpline staff | Triage scripts and tool recommendations | Access Now Helpline, Coalition Against Stalkerware |
| Newsroom security teams | Fast checks for reporters and sources | CPJ, Freedom of the Press Foundation, OCCRP |
| Journalist and NGO networks | Distribution to high-risk users | CPJ, Article 19, Front Line Defenders |
| Domestic-abuse advocates | Stalkerware guidance and safe referral paths | NNEDV Safety Net, local shelters |
| Libraries and universities | Public-interest training material | Journalism and security faculties |

## Ground rules

- **Do not imply endorsement.** Say "maintained by", not "endorsed by" an organization.
- **Do not send to at-risk individuals unprompted.** Share through the organizations that support them.
- **Do not collect recipient data.** No tracking links, no forms.
- **Respect local context.** Offer the handout for translation rather than assuming English is usable.
- **Correct fast.** If a recipient finds an error, fix it and tell them.

## Follow-up

One follow-up after two weeks, then stop. Ask one question: "Is there anything missing for the people you support?" Feedback that names a gap is more useful than a thank-you.
