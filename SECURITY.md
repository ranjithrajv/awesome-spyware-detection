# Security Policy

## Scope

This repository is a **curated list**. It does not ship or maintain the tools it links to, and it runs no service beyond a static GitHub Pages handout.

This policy covers:

- Malicious or hijacked links in this repository.
- Content that could put a person at risk, such as victim-identifying details.
- Factual errors with a safety impact, such as a tool described as working on a platform it does not support.
- Vulnerabilities in this repository's own automation (the index generator and GitHub Actions workflows).

It does **not** cover vulnerabilities in listed third-party tools. Report those to the tool's maintainers; we will update or remove an entry once a public advisory exists.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:

**https://github.com/ranjithrajv/awesome-spyware-detection/security/advisories/new**

Please include:

- What is affected (entry name, URL, or workflow).
- Why it is a safety or security problem, not just a typo.
- Steps to reproduce or a reference to a public report, where applicable.
- Whether you want attribution.

Do **not** open a public issue for anything that could expose a targeted person or an unpatched tool. If you are unsure, use the private advisory form.

## What to expect

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 7 days |
| Triage and severity assessment | 14 days |
| Removal or correction of actively harmful content | 24–72 hours where feasible |
| Public credit | On request, after the fix ships |

This is a volunteer-maintained project; these are best-effort targets, not contractual SLAs.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research and reporting under this policy will not be pursued or reported. Do not access, download, or redistribute victim data, and do not test against third-party systems.

## Out of scope

- Disagreement with editorial inclusion decisions.
- Requests to add tools (use the resource issue template).
- Vulnerabilities in third-party software listed here.
- Reports that require us to contact a vendor or government on your behalf.
